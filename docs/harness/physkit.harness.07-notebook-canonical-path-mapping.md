## 3. Mapping conventions

- Broad roots come from the human-supplied provisional root set.
- General mathematical and numerical methods use `foundations/`; domain notebooks remain in their physical domain even when computational methods are central.
- `staging/` is used only for unresolved invalid JSON, exact/probable duplication, repair assessment, or identity disposition.
- Saved-error notebooks retain a subject-based proposal when their intellectual classification is clear.
- Filenames are lowercase, descriptive, hyphen-separated, and avoid lifecycle labels and accidental course numbering. Stable filenames are retained where possible.
- A concrete proposed path does not decide canonical status, duplicate survival, migration, or lifecycle.
