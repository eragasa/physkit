# Mathematics

The implementation evaluates the canonical partition-function convention

$$
\lambda_T=\frac{h}{\sqrt{2\pi m k_BT}}
$$

for positive mass $m$ and absolute temperature $T$. For nonnegative number
density $n$, it separately reports

$$
\chi=n\lambda_T^3.
$$

The complete analytical derivation and its relation to the de Broglie relation
are maintained in the [thermal de Broglie wavelength lecture
note](../../../../../../../../../lecture-notes/statistical-mechanics/thermal-de-broglie-wavelength/index.md).
