# Module `projectkoios.physkit.thermal.statmech.radiation.blackbody`

## Current responsibility

This module owns wavelength-domain blackbody spectral energy density and its
Wien and Rayleigh--Jeans asymptotic forms. The represented density has dimensions
of energy per volume per wavelength; it is not spectral radiance or surface
flux.
