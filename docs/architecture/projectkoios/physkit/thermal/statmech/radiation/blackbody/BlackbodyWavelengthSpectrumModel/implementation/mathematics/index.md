# Mathematics

For $x=hc/(\lambda k_BT)$, the implementation evaluates

$$
u_\lambda=\frac{8\pi hc}{\lambda^5}\frac{1}{e^x-1}.
$$

Its represented limiting forms are

$$
u_\lambda^{\mathrm{Wien}}=\frac{8\pi hc}{\lambda^5}e^{-x}
$$

and

$$
u_\lambda^{\mathrm{RJ}}=\frac{8\pi k_BT}{\lambda^4}.
$$

The maintained [lecture note](../../../../../../../../../../lecture-notes/statistical-mechanics/blackbody-wavelength-spectrum/index.md)
explains the asymptotic derivations and represented quantity.
