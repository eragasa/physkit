# Mathematics

For a three-dimensional ideal gas at a planar interface, the positive
half-space Maxwell velocity moment gives

$$
J_+(P,T)=\frac{P}{\sqrt{2\pi m k_BT}}.
$$

The implementation evaluates

$$
J_{\mathrm{net}}
=\alpha_{\mathrm{evap}}J_+(P_{\mathrm{eq}},T)
-\alpha_{\mathrm{cond}}J_+(P_{\mathrm{ambient}},T),
$$

and signed mass flux $mJ_{\mathrm{net}}$. The complete three-dimensional
half-space derivation is maintained in the [lecture
note](../../../../../../../../../../lecture-notes/thermal-transport/phase-change/hertz-knudsen/index.md).
