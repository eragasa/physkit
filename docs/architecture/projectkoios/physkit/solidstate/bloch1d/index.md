# `projectkoios.physkit.solidstate.bloch1d`

This package owns one-dimensional Bloch physical models and their explicit
reference-cell representations.

- [`freeelectron`](freeelectron/index.md) owns the bare free-electron folded
  spectrum, exact Bloch modes, and its finite-difference Hamiltonian bridge to
  `projectkoios.physkit.periodic.pbc1d`.
- [`cosinepotential`](cosinepotential/index.md) owns a bare electron in one
  prescribed cosine potential and its represented Bloch bands.
