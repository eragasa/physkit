# `Piab2DAnalyticalSolution`

## Responsibility

`Piab2DAnalyticalSolution` is an immutable ResultObject correlating one
`Piab2D`, a nonempty immutable `(state_count, 2)` positive integer
quantum-number inventory, and a matching nondecreasing energy quantity.

Exact energy ties retain lexicographic pair order established by the evaluator.
